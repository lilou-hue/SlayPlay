Parlour, wall opened

backgrounds/parlor_wall_open

The room is faithful down to the props — same handless clock, same photo and folded cloth on the mantel, same dust sheets over the same two chairs, same rug, same brass knob on the door. The torn strip, the bare studs and the curl of paper on the floor all read exactly right, and the opening stays a black band rather than becoming a lit corridor.

One deviation. The brief wanted the camera close and square to the opened wall; it kept the wide parlour framing instead. That buys continuity at the cost of intimacy. Your call whether the trade is worth a second pass.

Box room, night

backgrounds/box_room_night

The first room here with no prior original, so it carries the house on palette alone — and it holds. Same green-grey walls, same cream skirting, same dark boards, same night window.

It got the detail that mattered: two rows of pinned paper, one at chest height and one much lower. That lower row is the whole point of the scene. The reel is threaded and about a third used, as asked.

Softer than the brief's hard cone with everything outside it black — the left half reads as ambient dim rather than torchlight. But the room reads, and it's a background with text over it.

Why these aren't on acceleration1st.com

That domain serves the production Sprites instance — port 3210, data in /var/lib/acceleration1st/sprites — which is deliberately still sitting on the 15 September snapshot, untouched, as asked.

Everything above was generated in the development instance on port 3211 with data in ~/sprites/data. That port binds to loopback only and is never put behind the authenticated proxy, so it has no web address at all. This page is how it gets out.

Seeing them in the game

All six are already installed as real game assets under public/assets/backgrounds/ and written into art/manifest.json. A preview build puts them in front of you in the scenes themselves rather than on a contact sheet.
